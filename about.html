About us

A focused public-records project, not a content mill.

The True Cost Project is positioned as a clean investigative hub: publish documented findings, show the underlying records, and give others a way to replicate or extend the work.

Purpose

Expose public support systems

Track grants, tax breaks, contracts, enforcement costs, and other public resources that prop up industrial animal agriculture.

Approach

Lead with records

Use budgets, minutes, invoices, correspondence, and footage requests to build accountability reporting that can stand up to scrutiny.

Audience

Readers and researchers

Publish in a format that works for journalists, donors, organizers, and local people who want to investigate similar patterns in their own areas.

Editorial posture

Credible, plain, and evidence-first.

The project aims to keep the tone plain and evidence-first: enough context to orient readers, enough documentation to support scrutiny, and enough clarity for others to build on the work.
